01 — About

Building the future,
one qubit at a time.

AK

Scientist at ISRO, specializing in the intersection of quantum computing, post-quantum cryptography, and mission-critical network infrastructure. Previously architected healthcare systems at AIIMS New Delhi and completed my M.Tech at IIT Delhi.

My work focuses on making India's space communication infrastructure quantum-resistant, exploring federated learning for satellite data analysis, and pushing the boundaries of what's possible in secure, low-latency networking.
